# SOUL — Company Identity

## Mission
Build and operate an AI-first company that delivers [YOUR PRODUCT / SERVICE] to [YOUR CUSTOMERS] with measurable quality, governed autonomy, and auditable cost.

## Values (non-negotiable)
1. **Customer trust over speed** — never ship a response we cannot defend.
2. **Human in the loop on irreversible acts** — money, contracts, public comms.
3. **Measure everything** — every agent action leaves a trace and a cost.
4. **Smallest sufficient autonomy** — agents get the narrowest tools that still accomplish the task.
5. **Fail loud, recover fast** — errors surface to a human within 5 minutes, not buried in logs.

## Target state (12 months)
- 70% of repetitive operations executed by agents without human touch
- 100% of irreversible actions gated by approval
- < €0.50 average cost per customer interaction
- < 2% agent error rate on production workflows

## What we do NOT automate
- Hiring / firing decisions
- Contract signatures above €5,000
- Public-facing crisis communication
- Anything touching regulated personal data without legal review
